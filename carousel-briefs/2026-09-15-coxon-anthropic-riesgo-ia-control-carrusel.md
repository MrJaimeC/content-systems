## Core angle

La versión viral mezcla dos personas y dos plazos. Jacob Coxon afirmó que quienes construyen IA creen que podría matar a toda la humanidad antes de acabar la década. Evan Hubinger, todavía responsable de alineamiento en Anthropic, estimó por separado una probabilidad superior al 10% durante la próxima década. Dario Amodei y Sam Altman pidieron ralentizar y reforzar la supervisión, pero ninguno validó el “10% antes de 2030” como dato científico.

## Verified corrections

- Coxon worked in pretraining across OpenAI and Anthropic, then resigned from Anthropic after roughly four months.
- Coxon’s viral statement used “by the end of the decade” without attaching the widely repeated >10% figure to that exact deadline.
- Hubinger supplied the explicit >10% estimate and said “within the next decade,” a different horizon.
- Amodei asked the industry to pace frontier capability development and committed Anthropic to embedded third-party evaluators.
- Altman/OpenAI supported pacing, independent evaluation and slowing or stopping when safety bars are not met; he did not endorse the 10% estimate.
- The International AI Safety Report says current systems do not yet have the combined capabilities required for loss of control and that probability and timing remain unusually uncertain.
