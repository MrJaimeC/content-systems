Objective:

Turn the OpenAI GPT-6.1 Sol launch into a practical carousel about model choice, cost control and when to use near-frontier capability instead of always choosing the most expensive model.

Core angle:

OpenAI did not just release another model. GPT-6.1 Sol brings near-Astra performance down into everyday professional work at a lower cost, but higher capability also needs clearer permissions, review and model-selection discipline.

Audience:

Creators, consultants, small businesses and operators who use ChatGPT, Codex, automations and AI workflows but do not want to burn budget by using the wrong model for every task.

Visual notes:

- The main brand must be OpenAI/ChatGPT.
- Use `GPT-6.1 SOL` as a clean product card; do not invent a custom Sol logo beyond official OpenAI/ChatGPT marks and model-name typography.
- Use model selector panels, cost calculators, workbench screens, task boards, permission/review gates and practical matrices.
- Jaime appears on slide 1 and slide 8 only, with distinct poses.

Slide plan:

1. OpenAI lanzó Sol 6.1: "El modelo que acerca Astra al trabajo diario."
2. Qué cambia de verdad: not another chat model, but complex work at lower cost.
3. Sol 6.1 vs Astra: maximum capability vs near-Astra cost-effective work.
4. The economic data: 2 USD input / 10 USD output per 1M tokens in standard API pricing; focus on cost per task.
5. Where to use it: code, analysis, long documents, research, automations, tool-heavy workflows.
6. Where not to use it: simple replies, easy classification, low-risk work or tasks where Luna/cheaper models are enough.
7. The serious part: more capability means more permissions, more risk and more review.
8. CTA: "COMENTA SELECTOR" for the model selector resource.

Caption direction:

OpenAI acaba de mover la parte interesante: no es solo tener el modelo más potente, sino elegir cuándo usarlo. Sol 6.1 puede tener sentido para trabajo complejo a volumen, pero si lo usas para todo, quemas presupuesto.

Resource concept:

`Selector OpenAI: cuándo usar Sol 6.1, Astra o un modelo barato`.
