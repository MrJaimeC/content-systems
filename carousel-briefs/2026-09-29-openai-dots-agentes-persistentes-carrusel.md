## Core Angle

OpenAI Dots is not just another chat feature. It is OpenAI's move toward persistent agents inside ChatGPT: a dot can keep context, use memory, work with connected apps, perform scheduled or proactive work, and follow action rules.

Spanish hook: "OpenAI acaba de convertir ChatGPT en alguien que puede seguir trabajando cuando tu no estas."

## Audience

Jaime's audience: creators, consultants and small businesses that use AI but need a practical permissions model before giving agents access to apps, email, Slack, files, calendar or coding tasks.
