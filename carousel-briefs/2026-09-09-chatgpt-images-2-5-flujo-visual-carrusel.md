## Objective
Explicar que el valor del lanzamiento no es solo generar imágenes, sino corregir con precisión, mantener consistencia y reducir esperas durante la producción visual.

## Core angle
Generar ya era fácil. El cuello de botella era revisar una pieza sin reconstruirla. ChatGPT Images 2.5 ataca esa fase con edición más precisa, consistencia entre iteraciones y hasta 50% menos latencia frente a Images 2.0.

## Official sources
- OpenAI launch, 8 September 2026: https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-images-2-5/
- OpenAI brand guidelines: https://openai.com/brand/

## Verified facts
- OpenAI states latency is reduced by up to 50% compared with Images 2.0.
- The launch highlights sharper details, precise targeted edits, identity preservation and multi-turn consistency.
- ChatGPT adds Sketch, templates, image comments and prompt sharing.
- The launch is available across ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work and Codex on desktop, mobile and web.
- API variants: Flare is the faster default for most applications; Sunburst adds precision for premium workflows and takes longer.
- OpenAI applies C2PA metadata and invisible watermarking.
