## Core Angle

Claude Sonnet 5.5 is not the "most expensive flagship" story. It is the model many teams may use as the operational default: fast, 1M context, 128K output, adaptive thinking, $2/$10 per MTok, and up to 30% lower task cost for most work according to Anthropic.

Spanish hook: "Anthropic acaba de lanzar el Claude que vas a usar todos los dias."

## Audience

Creators, consultants, small business owners and operators using AI for daily work: summaries, document review, lead classification, content drafts, support, lightweight coding and automations.

## Slide Plan

1. Hook: "Anthropic acaba de lanzar el Claude que vas a usar todos los dias". Badges: 30% mas rapido, hasta 30% menos coste por tarea, $2/$10.
2. What is Sonnet 5.5: latest active Sonnet, released September 28, 2026, 1M context, 128K output, fast latency, adaptive thinking.
3. Difference versus Haiku, Opus and Fable: Sonnet as default daily model, Opus for complex prolonged work, Fable for extremes, Haiku for cheap simple work.
4. Effort matters: between_tools/low/medium/high/max mindset, choose effort per task rather than always maxing out.
5. Price is not the full story: price per token plus fewer steps/retries/corrections equals cost per finished task.
6. Where to use it in a real business: calls, leads, docs, support, content drafts, automations with context.
7. When not to use it: Haiku may be enough, Opus/Fable may be justified for complex decisions, sensitive actions need human review.
8. CTA: Comenta MATRIZ. Resource: "Matriz para elegir modelo Claude sin tirar dinero."

## Caption Direction

Focus on model choice as an operating decision: do not always pay for the top model; choose by task, risk, volume, reasoning depth and review cost.
