## Brand Direction

- Brand: `@jaime_cabadas`
- Language: Spanish.
- Tone: aggressive, direct, premium, human. Use hypeable hooks with tension and consequence, but no empty clickbait or "AI bro" voice.
- Palette: warm cream / marfil, near-black / dark brown, terracotta / copper accents, beige / taupe / olive support details.
- Layout: editorial desk, notes, checklists, paper systems, visual frameworks, clean high-contrast typography.
- Persona usage: use Jaime's real image as a reference when generating slides that include him. Keep likeness realistic and professional.
- Branding references show the target look: premium Instagram carousel, warm cream background, heavy near-black uppercase headings, copper/terracotta emphasis, thin line icons, small slide counter, `@jaime_cabadas` in the top right, realistic Jaime portrait composited with practical desk/whiteboard/post-it scenes. Final slides must be vertical 3:4.
- The strongest visual references are the full generated carousel folders, not isolated images. Treat each folder as a complete carousel system: slide rhythm, layout, typography, icon language, Jaime integration, UI mockups, paper props, CTA, and final slide behavior.
- CTA: every carousel must point to redeeming the lead magnet. Use a comment keyword plus `https://jaimecabadas.com/comunidad` when appropriate.
- Visual variety rule: do not repeat Jaime in the same pose or repeat the same desk/object setup across many slides. Vary body angle, crop, camera distance, expression, hand position, location inside the composition, and whether Jaime appears at all. Use different but coherent props per slide.
- Dynamic presence rule: Jaime should appear only when it strengthens the slide. Do not force him into slide 1, slide 5, slide 8 or any fixed pattern. For tool/product/news carousels, some slides may be brand-led, UI-led, document-led, workflow-led or logo-led with no Jaime at all.
- Brand hierarchy rule: if the carousel is about one main tool or company, that brand must visually lead the carousel. Use its real logo or official-looking brand treatment prominently where practical. Secondary tools should not visually compete unless the angle is genuinely about comparison or ecosystem.
- Theme relevance rule: backgrounds and props must match the topic being explained. Email topics should show inboxes, reply drafts, tone controls, client-message cards and approval/revision cues. Automation topics should show workflow maps, tools, triggers, dashboards and process boards. Content topics should show calendars, scripts, cameras, microphones and editing boards.
- Tool logo rule: when the slide talks about specific real tools, include real logo assets or clearly recognizable official product marks where practical. Before generating a carousel with named brands/tools, search for official logo/product icon assets or credible real logo files, save them under `/Users/bumblebee/.openclaw/workspace/refs/logos/`, convert SVGs to PNG when needed, and pass those files as Image 2 references. Do not invent fake tool logos. Use a clean app card with the real tool name only when no reliable logo asset can be found or Image 2 clearly deforms the mark.
- News carousel rule: before choosing a topic from the topic bank, check recent important AI, automation, no-code and creator-tool news. Prioritize official company posts, product release notes, reputable tech press, and credible primary sources. If one story is clearly highly relevant to Jaime's audience, use that story as the carousel topic instead of an evergreen topic. Include the source URLs in the brief. Keep the angle practical: what changed, why it matters, what Jaime's audience should do with it, and what resource/lead magnet helps them act.
- Anti-repetition rule: before recommending news, read the daily news blacklist and prior briefs/outbox briefs. Reject any exact repeated URL for 30 days and any repeated macro-angle for 10 days, even when the source/company differs. In the preapproval, include one line explaining why the recommended news is not blocked by recent topics.
- Brand/logo accuracy rule for news: when a news carousel mentions ChatGPT/OpenAI, Claude/Anthropic, Google, Perplexity, Meta, xAI, Make, n8n, Zapier, Notion, Adobe, Canva or any other real brand, use official or credible real logo assets as generation references wherever possible. If the main company/tool is the protagonist, do not skip this step. If exact rendering is unreliable after using a real asset reference, use a clean card with the real brand/tool name and source attribution rather than a made-up logo, and mention the limitation in the brief.
- Hook aggression rule: carousel angles and hooks must include the main brand, tool name, metric, or keyword when the topic has one. Prefer sharp, high-tension framing that makes the change feel urgent and specific: `Adobe acaba de decir la parte incomoda`, `Canva Code acaba de matar el PDF lead magnet`, `OpenAI no lanzo una funcion: movio el tablero`. Avoid soft generic hooks such as `La IA ya no es ventaja` when a stronger branded hook is available.
- Hype discipline rule: make the carousel more punchy, visual and shareable by using stakes, contrast, numbers, brand names, and clear "what changes now" language. Keep the substance practical and credible; never promise guaranteed money, instant results, or fake drama unsupported by the source.

## Complete Carousel Style Analysis

Before generating any new carousel, inspect the complete reference folders in `/Users/bumblebee/Desktop/OpenClaw Generated Images/`.

Use these patterns:

- Output is a complete Image 2 slide, not a manually composed design. Do not generate backgrounds and then overlay text locally except for emergency debugging previews.
- Each `slide-XX.png` should already contain final typography, visual props, UI mockups, Jaime portrait when needed, brand handle, slide counter, icons, notes, cards and CTA inside the generated image.
- Format: vertical 3:4 for every final slide, for example `1080x1440`, `1200x1600`, or any equivalent 3:4 ratio. Do not output 4:5 slides.
- Slide counter appears top left in a rounded pill, for example `01/08`, with a thin terracotta horizontal line.
- `@jaime_cabadas` appears top right, small and unobtrusive.
- Main typography: bold uppercase, near-black, with terracotta emphasis words such as `IA`, `CONTENIDO`, `VENTAS`, `AUTOMATIZACION`, `DELEGALAS`.
- Background: warm cream/marfil, editorial, clean, high-end desk/workspace scenes.
- Recurring visual props: laptop/tablet UI, paper notes, checklists, sticky notes, process maps, notebooks, coffee, plants, microphone/camera when content creation appears.
- Jaime appears as part of the scene, realistically integrated, not pasted as a cutout.
- Avoid visual repetition: across the 8 slides, rotate scenes and prop families. Do not make every slide a similar Jaime-at-desk composition. Some slides can be UI-first, some diagram-first, some document-first, some portrait-led, some tool-logo-led, and some process-board-led.
- Slide sequence pattern:
  - Slide 1: strong hook + Jaime portrait + contextual wall/desk elements.
  - Slide 2: first principle or mindset shift with process diagram/table.
  - Slides 3-7: grouped categories, each with concrete bullet list and matching visual system.
  - Slide 8: decisive action/CTA, Jaime portrait, concise steps, save/comment prompt.
- Text should be concise enough for Image 2 to render legibly. Prefer short blocks, numbered lists and simple labels.
- Use Spanish with controlled hype: aggressive hook, clear brand/keyword, practical body, premium finish.

Avoid these mistakes:

- Do not send raw Image 2 generations if they are reference mosaics, moodboards or base images.
- Do not crop a 2x2 generated mosaic and pretend it is a finished carousel.
- Do not build the final carousel by placing text boxes over unrelated generated backgrounds.
- Do not reuse an existing outbox, ZIP, PDF or slide pack unless it includes `generation-manifest.json` proving `model: openai/gpt-image-2` for the 8 delivered slides.
- Do not run legacy local/PIL/template builders for final slides. Scripts that use `ImageDraw`, local text overlays, HTML screenshots, SVG templates or ReportLab-generated slide imagery are allowed only for debugging/supporting documents, never as final carousel slide generation.
- Do not use generic file names such as `test-carrusel...` in final delivery.
- Do not package confusing artifacts. Final delivery should contain clean names and the complete carousel.

## Image 2 Provenance Gate

Every final carousel package must have machine-checkable Image 2 provenance.

- Required manifest: `generation-manifest.json` in both the output folder and ZIP.
- Required manifest fields:
  - `model`: exactly `openai/gpt-image-2`.
  - `tool`: the concrete generation path, for example `openclaw infer image edit`.
  - `raw_dir`: where the raw Image 2 outputs were saved.
  - `final_dir`: where the delivery slides were standardized/packaged.
  - `slides`: exactly `slide-01.png` through `slide-08.png`.
- Before delivering, inspect the manifest and confirm it matches the current package. If the manifest is missing, names a different model, or points to another package, stop and regenerate with Image 2.
- Reusing a previous package is forbidden unless its manifest passes this gate and the approved topic/angle matches the current approval.
- Preferred live command path: run `scripts/generate_carousel_image2_package.py` with an Image 2 generator script, then validate the outbox before delivery.

## Daily Flow

The scheduled daily task must start with an approval checkpoint before generating the full package.

1. Check recent important AI, automation, no-code and creator-tool news from official or reputable sources. Use a broad scan, not a single feed: official company blogs, product changelogs, reputable tech/business media, AI tool blogs, no-code platforms, creator economy sources and major developer platforms.
2. Read the daily news blacklist, topic bank, prior brief files, and outbox brief files.
3. Build a short "recently burned" list of exact URLs and macro-angles. Exact repeated URLs remain blocked for 30 days unless Jaime explicitly asks to revisit them. Repeated macro-angles are no longer an automatic block: label the repetition risk and find a sharper, concrete new angle when the underlying fact is fresh.
4. Prepare exactly 5 distinct news candidates before considering the topic bank. Each candidate must include:
   - source URL;
   - publication/date context when available;
   - credibility note;
   - one-line factual summary;
   - practical angle for Jaime's audience;
   - potency score from 1-10;
   - repetition risk: `BAJO`, `MEDIO`, or `ALTO`;
   - why it could make a carousel today.
5. The five candidates should be meaningfully different. Prefer diversity across product launches, agent/workflow changes, automation tools, creator/business tools, regulation/platform shifts, pricing/cost changes, developer tooling and market moves. Avoid giving Jaime five variants of the same company or the same generic "agents are coming" story.
6. Pick one strong evergreen backup topic from the topic bank only as a fallback after the five news candidates.
7. Send Jaime a short approval message before generating anything:
   - heading: `5 NOTICIAS CANDIDATAS`;
   - each candidate's source URL, potency score, repetition risk and recommended carousel angle;
   - your own ranked recommendation: `yo haria la #X`;
   - optional evergreen backup topic;
   - explicit note that approved generation will use `openai/gpt-image-2` and manifest verification;
   - exact question asking Jaime which of the five news items he chooses, or whether he prefers the backup.
8. Stop after the approval message. Do not generate slides, lead magnet, ZIP, PDF or contact sheet until Jaime explicitly chooses a candidate or approves another route.

## Lead Magnet Rules

- It must solve the same problem as the carousel.
- It should feel like the practical next step after reading the carousel.
- Assume the lead magnet is consumed by people who are already inside the community. Do not write it as a lead-capture asset and do not include a sales CTA asking them to join.
- Resource filenames must not include the `lead-magnet-` prefix from 2026-07-22 onward. Use asset-specific names such as `checklist-{slug}.pdf`, `guia-{slug}.pdf`, `plantilla-{slug}.pdf`, `mapa-{slug}.pdf`, or `auditoria-{slug}.pdf`. Keep legacy validation compatible with older `lead-magnet-` packages.
- After Telegram delivery, upload only the PDFs to Jaime's Drive folder with `rclone` remote `jaime_carousel_drive`: `carrusel-8-slides.pdf` and the resource PDF. Do not upload ZIPs to Drive.
- Preferred command: `scripts/upload_carousel_pdfs_to_drive.sh outbox/{delivery-folder}`.
- The current `rclone` remote uses rclone's shared Google Drive client ID; it works now, but create a Jaime-owned OAuth client ID during 2026 for long-term reliability.
- Use concrete sections: quick diagnosis, checklist, prompts, worksheet, examples, next action.
- Name it as a redeemable asset, for example:
  - `Guía`
  - `Checklist`
  - `Plantilla`
  - `Mapa`
  - `Auditoría rápida`
- CTA wording should match the asset:
  - `Comenta GUÍA y te paso la guía`
  - `Comenta CHECKLIST y te paso la checklist`
  - `Comenta PLANTILLA y te paso la plantilla`
- At the end of the lead magnet, include a short community reminder instead of a join CTA:
  - remind readers that the community is on WhatsApp.
  - frame the benefit as access to Jaime's real cases, day-to-day building process and exclusive resources.
  - include the community link as a reminder/reference, not as a hard sell.
  - use the current community URL from the newsletter runbook if a WhatsApp link is needed.
